# Brag plan: Passalong tutor recruiting ad

## Answers

- **What is it?** Passalong (passalonglb.com) is student-to-student university tutoring in Lebanon. Right now it only recruits tutors. It opens to students later.
- **Who is it for?** Senior university students who have already passed a course and could tutor it.
- **What sets it apart?** Students contact tutors directly with no middleman. Tutors set their own price. The first month is free, and it only starts on the day the site opens to students.
- **Most impressive claim:** "Apply now and be one of our first tutors."
- **Visual hook:** a wordplay on the name: "You passed the course." / "Now pass it along."
- **Real UI to show:** the home hero, a tap on "Become a tutor", the filled-in apply form scrolling by, the confirm box, and "Thanks for applying!" with "Your first month is free".
- **Tone:** `default` with some `polished` restraint: confident, warm and clean. It's a real recruiting ad, not a joke.
- **Share caption:** Passed a course? Teach it. Passalong is looking for its first tutors.
